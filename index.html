Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or to pay?

No. You can use every tool without signing up or paying. Your settings and history are saved only in your own browser.

What is the Viral Scene Finder?

It is a search tool for video editors. Type a movie, series, anime, game, character or scene name and it looks through a built-in library to suggest specific scenes that tend to work as Shorts and Reels, with a hook, edit and reaction idea for each. The results are suggestions, not a guarantee of views.

Does ShortForm AI use a live AI model?

No. The Viral Scene Finder and the title, hashtag and tags tools run on a built-in library of scenes and rule-based templates inside your browser. They do not call a live AI model, so the suggestions are curated ideas rather than generated analysis. “AI” is part of the product name.

Do Instagram Reels use tags?

No. Instagram has no tags field. Reels are found through hashtags, caption keywords and on-screen text, so use the Hashtag Generator for Instagram. YouTube tags are a separate, YouTube-only feature.

How many hashtags should I use on YouTube Shorts?

YouTube shows the first three hashtags above the title and ignores all hashtags on a video if you use more than 15. ShortForm AI can give you the 3 best SEO hashtags or up to 15 relevant ones.

How long can a YouTube Shorts title be?

Up to 100 characters. The Title Generator keeps YouTube titles within that limit, including #Shorts.

Which movies, games and series are covered?

Marvel and DC films, Star Wars, Harry Potter, The Lord of the Rings, Pixar and Disney animation, popular anime and series such as Stranger Things and Game of Thrones, and big games like God of War, GTA and Elden Ring. The library keeps growing.

Can ShortForm AI guarantee a Short will go viral?

No tool can. The suggestions focus on scenes with strong hooks and clear edit moments, but results depend on your edit, your audience and timing.

ShortForm AI (also written Short Form AI) is built for short-form video editors. Scene, title and hashtag suggestions are ideas to adapt, not guarantees of views.
